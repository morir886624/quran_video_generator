# Privacy Policy — Quran Video Studio

**Effective Date:** September 2026  
**Application:** Quran Video Studio (`com.quran.videomaker`)  
**Publisher:** Quran Video Studio  

This Privacy Policy explains how Quran Video Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your data and privacy.

---

### 1. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not require accounts, logins, emails, phone numbers, or passwords.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** No tracking SDKs, device fingerprinting, or behavior tracking cookies are used.
- **No Advertising:** The app contains no ads, ad banners, or tracking for marketing purposes.
- **No Financial Data:** The app is free and does not process payments or collect billing details.

---

### 2. Network Requests & Third-Party APIs
To display Quranic text and recite verses, the app makes secure HTTPS calls to reputable, public APIs:
- **api.quran.com:** To fetch Quranic verses, chapter metadata, and translations.
- **audio.qurancdn.com:** To stream Quran recitations.

These requests only transmit the requested chapter or verse indices and do not transmit any personal user identifiers.

---

### 3. Media & Storage Permissions
All video rendering occurs **client-side directly on your device** using Web Audio and Canvas engines.
- **Saving Videos:** Videos are saved locally to your device storage (`Documents` or cache).
- **Sharing Videos:** Videos shared via the Android Share sheet are processed directly by your operating system to your chosen app (e.g., WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram). No videos are ever uploaded to our servers.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)
The app is safe and suitable for all age groups, including families and children. We do not collect any personal information from children or adults.
