Privacy Policy

Quran Video Studio • Effective Date: September 2026
Google Play Compliant

This Privacy Policy explains how Quran Video Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. We deeply value your privacy and are committed to ensuring our users enjoy a private, safe, and transparent experience.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Quran Video Studio is designed to respect your digital privacy:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not employ third-party tracking pixels, device fingerprinting, or user behavioral analytics.
  • No Advertisements: The application does not serve targeted ads or share advertising identifiers (GAID).
  • No Payment Data: The application does not process or collect financial or credit card information.

2. Third-Party Services & Network Access

To provide authentic Quranic content, the app makes secure HTTPS connections to public, open-source Quran APIs:

  • Quran Foundation API (api.quran.com): Used exclusively to retrieve Quran text, translations, and chapter metadata.
  • Quran CDN (audio.qurancdn.com): Used to stream recitation audio by reputable Qaris.

These network requests transmit only technical protocol parameters (such as the verse numbers requested) and do not send personal user data.

3. Device Storage & Media Permissions

When you generate a video using Quran Video Studio, the video rendering takes place entirely on your device using client-side canvas and Web Audio processing.

  • Local Storage: If you choose to save or download a video, the file is written to your local device storage (e.g. Documents or app cache).
  • Sharing: If you trigger the native Share Sheet, your device’s operating system manages the file transfer to your selected destination (such as WhatsApp, Instagram, or Files). The app does not upload your created videos to any external server.

4. Children's Privacy

Quran Video Studio contains religious and educational content appropriate for all audiences, including children. We strictly comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be published with an updated effective date.
